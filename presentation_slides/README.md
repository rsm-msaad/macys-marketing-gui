# Final Presentation — June 4

Our final presentation is a **live walkthrough** using the deployed web app, not a static slide deck.

**Presentation URL:** [macysai.vercel.app/story](https://macysai.vercel.app/story)

The `/story` storyboard tab walks through the full campaign workflow with hand-drawn sketches and narrated steps. During the presentation, we demo the live app alongside the storyboard to show:

1. **Current business process** — M1 swimlane diagram and pain points
2. **AI-supported workflow diagram** — color-coded M4 redesign (also in `ai_process_design.pdf`)
3. **Working UI with realistic input** — live demo: create a campaign on `/start`, step through the 10-step workflow, see real AI output from the compliance, brief, and routing skills
4. **Data and evidence used by the AI** — Evidence screen at `/evidence` showing RAG documents, MCP tool calls, data sources, and prior step outputs
5. **Human review step** — Review screen at `/review` with Approve, Edit, Reject, Rerun, and Escalate actions demonstrated live
6. **Time, cost, and quality estimates** — from `estimates.md`, presented during the storyboard walkthrough
7. **Face-validity check** — why the analysis and AI results are reasonable (from `face_validity.md`)
8. **Testing AI performance** — actual test results from `test_report.md` and the eval dashboard at `/evals`
9. **What we would improve with more time** — discussed during Q&A and documented in the storyboard
