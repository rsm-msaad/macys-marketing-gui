# Compliance Flag Examples

**Document ID:** COMP-EX-2026-001
**Owner:** Marketing Compliance, Brand Review
**Last updated:** March 4, 2026
**Audience:** Marketing, Creative, Copywriting

## Purpose

This document is a reference of real compliance flags raised on past Macys campaigns. Each example shows the flagged copy, the rule that was tripped, and the fix that landed in production. Use this when drafting new creative to anticipate where reviews tend to push back.

## Examples

### Example 1, banned word "last chance"

* **Flagged copy**: "Last chance on Spring Beauty, ends Sunday."
* **Rule tripped**: BRAND-GL-2026-001, banned word list.
* **Fix**: "Spring Beauty, ends Sunday." (urgency carried by the date, not the phrase.)

### Example 2, "up to" missing minimum

* **Flagged copy**: "Up to 50 percent off Home Refresh."
* **Rule tripped**: BRAND-GL-2026-001 pricing language plus LEGAL-DIS-2026-002 FTC representative pricing.
* **Fix**: "Up to 50 percent off Home Refresh, starting at 20 percent off."

### Example 3, compare at price without 90 day basis

* **Flagged copy**: "Compare at $129, now $59."
* **Rule tripped**: LEGAL-DIS-2026-002, post 2024 NY AG settlement on compare at pricing.
* **Fix**: Either confirm and document the prior 90 day offer at $129, or change to "Manufacturer suggested retail price $129, our price $59" with the corresponding disclaimer.

### Example 4, "save big" headline

* **Flagged copy**: "Save big on Bedding this weekend."
* **Rule tripped**: BRAND-GL-2026-001, pricing language ("save big" not allowed as a primary headline).
* **Fix**: "Bedding refresh, 30 percent off this weekend."

### Example 5, financing offer without TILA language

* **Flagged copy**: "Zero percent financing for 12 months on purchases over $499."
* **Rule tripped**: LEGAL-DIS-2026-002, Macys Credit Card Truth in Lending disclosure.
* **Fix**: Same headline with the required disclaimer added in the same creative unit, including "Subject to credit approval" and the deferred interest language.

### Example 6, influencer post missing disclosure

* **Flagged copy**: Instagram post from partner influencer, "Loving my new Macys finds for fall."
* **Rule tripped**: LEGAL-DIS-2026-002, endorsement disclosure (FTC).
* **Fix**: Same post with "#MacysPartner" and "Paid partnership" tag visible in the post itself, not buried in the caption.

### Example 7, restricted asset reused

* **Flagged copy**: A Holiday gift guide reused a Lancome influencer collaboration asset for a non Beauty placement.
* **Rule tripped**: DAM-TAG-2025-005, restricted asset usage outside agreement.
* **Fix**: Asset pulled, replaced with a Macys owned alternative. Asset reuse request routed through Creative Operations for future shoots.

### Example 8, Spanish call to action missing accent

* **Flagged copy**: "Compra ya y ahorra mas."
* **Rule tripped**: LOC-STYLE-2025-002, neutral Latin American Spanish, missing accent.
* **Fix**: Same line corrected with the accented form of mas (the comparative adverb, accented). Two stage Spanish review now required per the Q4 2025 retro action item.

### Example 9, MAP brand discounted

* **Flagged copy**: "Levi's, 25 percent off this weekend."
* **Rule tripped**: PRICE-RULES-2026-001, Levi's is a MAP enforced brand.
* **Fix**: Creative pulled before launch. Promotional language replaced with "Shop Levi's, new arrivals." Pricing Strategy notified Merchandising.

### Example 10, sweepstakes missing rules

* **Flagged copy**: "Enter to win a $500 Macys gift card, sign up for emails today."
* **Rule tripped**: LEGAL-DIS-2026-002, sweepstakes required language (no purchase necessary, void where prohibited, odds, end date and time zone, full rules link).
* **Fix**: Sweepstakes mechanic relaunched with full rules linked, all required language present, plus the bonded state filings handled by Legal.

## How to Use This Document

Run a quick scan of any draft headline and disclaimer block against this list before submitting for Brand or Legal review. Catching these issues at the draft stage saves an average of 1.5 review days per campaign based on the Q4 2025 to Q1 2026 data set.
