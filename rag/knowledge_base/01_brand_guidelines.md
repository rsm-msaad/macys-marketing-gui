# Macys Brand Guidelines

**Document ID:** BRAND-GL-2026-001
**Owner:** Brand Marketing Council
**Last updated:** January 22, 2026
**Audience:** Marketing, Creative, Copywriting, Agency Partners

## Voice and Tone

Macys speaks to the customer the way a trusted friend would. Warm, confident, occasionally playful. The voice should feel like someone who genuinely loves the product and wants the customer to feel good walking out of the store or closing their cart. Avoid corporate stiffness, avoid hard sell. The customer should never feel pressured.

Tone shifts by occasion. Holiday campaigns lean joyful and celebratory. Beauty leans intimate and discovery oriented. Home leans practical and aspirational. Workwear leans capable and modern.

## Approved Taglines

The following taglines are pre approved for use in any campaign without further sign off:

* The Magic of Macys
* Find Your Magic
* Star Rewards, Every Day
* New In Store, New Online

Any tagline outside this list requires Brand Marketing Council review before it appears in production creative.

## Banned Words and Phrases

The following terms are not permitted in any Macys campaign creative. They conflict with brand positioning or have been flagged by Legal in prior reviews.

* cheap
* basic
* last chance
* clearance blowout
* dirt cheap
* unbeatable
* lowest prices anywhere
* nobody beats Macys

If a campaign requires urgency language, use "ends Sunday", "limited time", or "while supplies last" instead.

## Pricing Language

All pricing claims must follow the standard format below. This format was negotiated with Legal in 2024 and is required for compliance with state consumer protection laws.

* For range claims, write "Up to 40 percent off" never "40% off" alone.
* For tiered claims, always include the minimum: "Up to 40 percent off, starting at 10 percent off."
* Never use "free" without specifying conditions in the same line of copy.
* Never use "save big" or "huge savings" as the primary headline.

## Visual Standards

The Macys star is the only protected logo mark. It must appear in either the official red (PMS 186, hex C8102E) or black, never recolored. Minimum clear space around the star is equal to one star height. The star may not be tilted, outlined, or filled with imagery.

## Color Palette

* Primary red: hex C8102E
* Charcoal: hex 2E2E2E
* Soft cream: hex F5EFE6
* Accent gold for Holiday: hex C4A052

Seasonal palettes are released quarterly by the Creative Council. Anything outside the approved palette requires explicit approval.

## Typography

* Headlines: Helvetica Neue Bold or the seasonal display font issued by Creative
* Body: Helvetica Neue Regular at 14 pt minimum on digital, 10 pt minimum on print
* Disclaimers: 8 pt minimum, never below the legal threshold for the channel

## Updates

This document is reviewed twice a year by the Brand Marketing Council. To request a change (a new tagline, a banned word addition, a color palette update), file a request through the Brand portal with a specific example and rationale. The Council meets the second Tuesday of each month.
