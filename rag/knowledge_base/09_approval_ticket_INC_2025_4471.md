The two revision cycles cost roughly three production days. Both were preventable. The "big savings" headline could have been caught at Brand review with a stronger initial revision rather than a comment plus approval. The comparative claim could have been caught at kickoff if Legal had been pre cleared on methodology. Both feed the retro action items.
