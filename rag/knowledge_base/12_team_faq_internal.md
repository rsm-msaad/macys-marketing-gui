# Internal Team FAQ

**Document ID:** FAQ-INT-2026-001
**Owner:** Marketing Operations
**Last updated:** April 1, 2026
**Audience:** Marketing team (all levels)

## Purpose

Common questions the Marketing Operations team gets every week. Answers reflect current practice, not aspirational policy. If something here conflicts with a posted policy document, the policy document wins, and please tell us so we can fix this FAQ.

## How long does VP approval usually take?

The SLA is 3 business days for standard tier and 5 business days for high spend (over $500K). In practice, the median is 2 days for standard and 4 days for high spend. The variability is mostly driven by whether you pre cleared the contentious items with Brand and Legal before submission. Campaigns that surface new questions at the VP stage routinely add 2 to 3 days. See TICKET-INC-2025-4471 for an example of a high spend ticket that took the full 5 days, and TICKET-INC-2026-0212 for a clean 2 day approval.

## Can I run a campaign without Legal review?

Yes for the baseline path (no spend over $500K, no regulated region, no new SKU, no sweepstakes, no comparative claim, no Star Rewards multiplier, no financing offer). In practice this is most short notice site refresh creative and routine seasonal placements. If any of those triggers apply, Legal is required.

## What if the DAM does not have the asset I need?

First, search by category and season tags rather than free text. Many assets are mis tagged and a tag based search finds things free text misses. If you still cannot find what you need, file a creative request through Creative Operations and flag the campaign date. Creative Ops will either pull from an upcoming shoot, request a small reshoot, or recommend an alternative direction. Lead time is typically 10 business days for new creative, 3 business days for a pull from an upcoming shoot.

## How do I escalate a stuck approval?

The escalation path is: reviewer's manager first, Marketing Operations approval queue lead second, VP of Marketing third, CMO only for issues stalled more than 10 business days. Before escalating, please confirm the reviewer is not out of office (the approval tool sometimes does not surface this). Most "stuck" approvals are reviewers on PTO whose backup was never set.

## Can I add Legal as a reviewer after submission?

Yes, edit the routing on the existing ticket. You do not need to resubmit. The clock resets only if a new revision is uploaded after Legal is added.

## What counts as a "new SKU" for Merchandising sign off?

A SKU that has not appeared in any prior Macys marketing creative within the previous 24 months. Re launches, new colorways of an existing item, and new sizes of an existing item do not require Merchandising sign off unless flagged in the seasonal Merchandising calendar.

## How do I find out if my brand is under MAP?

Check PRICE-RULES-2026-001 for the standing MAP list. Brands are added throughout the year so the standing list may be out of date. When in doubt, ask Merchandising before you write the promo headline.

## Who owns the post launch retro?

The campaign manager owns scheduling and notes. Marketing Operations owns the retro template, action item tracking, and the closeout. Retros are due within 4 weeks of campaign close.

## What if Legal pre cleared my copy but Brand later flagged it?

Legal sign off does not satisfy Brand sign off. Brand can flag a Legal cleared piece for voice, visual, or tagline issues. Both must approve. If you hit a conflict between Legal and Brand recommendations, escalate to the VP of Marketing for the call.

## Can I run a campaign in Puerto Rico without a Spanish version?

No. Spanish creative is required for any campaign running in Puerto Rico, Texas, or Florida. See LOC-STYLE-2025-002.

## How do I add a new banned word to the brand guidelines?

Submit a request to the Brand Marketing Council. Bring a specific example of the word in use, the rationale (legal risk, brand fit, customer feedback), and a proposed replacement. The Council reviews monthly.
