# Pricing and Promotion Rules

**Document ID:** PRICE-RULES-2026-001
**Owner:** Pricing Strategy, Merchandising
**Last updated:** January 30, 2026
**Audience:** Marketing, Merchandising, Creative

## Purpose

This document defines how promotional discounts can be combined, what categories are excluded from promotions, and how vendor MAP rules apply.

## Stackable Discounts

The default stacking order, where allowed, is:

1. Storewide promotion (for example, Friends and Family)
2. Category promotion (for example, 25 percent off Home)
3. Star Rewards member offer
4. Coupon code

When two storewide offers run simultaneously, only the higher discount applies. They do not stack.

## Phrase Meaning

"Cannot be combined with other offers" means the offer in question may not stack with any other promotional discount or coupon. It does not block the customer from applying Star Rewards points redemption (which is treated as currency, not a discount).

"Excludes select brands" requires a linked list of excluded brands in the same creative or footer.

## Exclusions

The following categories are typically excluded from storewide promotions. Confirm with Merchandising before each campaign, as exclusions can change by season.

* Fine Jewelry (above $1,000 ticket)
* Fragrance, with exceptions for promotional sets
* Beauty prestige (Lancome, Estee Lauder, Clinique, Dior Beauty, La Mer)
* Watches (Gucci, Tag Heuer)
* Designer Handbags (Coach, Kate Spade, Michael Kors above $500)
* Levi's
* Vendor restricted items flagged by Merchandising

## Brands Under Strict MAP

The following brands enforce minimum advertised price under their vendor agreements. Discounts on these brands require written Merchandising approval and are typically only available during vendor sponsored events.

* Levi's
* Coach (above $500 ticket)
* Lancome
* Estee Lauder
* Clinique
* La Mer
* Dior Beauty
* Tag Heuer

A campaign that violates MAP can result in vendor charge backs and, in repeat cases, loss of the vendor account. This is the single most cited reason for emergency creative pulls.

## Blackout Dates

Promotional creative may not run during the following windows without explicit Pricing Strategy approval:

* The week before and during a major brand event (for example, Lancome Friends and Family)
* Black Friday week, except for the approved Black Friday promotion plan
* Memorial Day week, except for the approved Memorial Day plan
* Vendor event windows published in the seasonal Merchandising calendar

## Markdown Cadence

Once an item enters markdown, the cadence follows the standard merch playbook:

1. First markdown at 20 percent off, held for 14 days
2. Second markdown at 33 percent off, held for 14 days
3. Final markdown at 50 percent off until sell through
4. Clearance below 50 percent only with Pricing Strategy approval

Markdown items may not be promoted as "new" or "just arrived" in marketing creative. Markdown items also may not appear in primary hero positions on the site or email without Pricing Strategy notice.

## Inventory Linked Promotions

If a promotion depends on inventory depth at a specific store or distribution center, the campaign owner must confirm inventory with Merchandising no earlier than 5 business days before launch. Inventory snapshots older than 5 days are considered stale.
