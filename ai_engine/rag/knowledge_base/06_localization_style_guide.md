# Localization Style Guide

**Document ID:** LOC-STYLE-2025-002
**Owner:** Localization Council, Regional Marketing
**Last updated:** October 4, 2025
**Audience:** Marketing, Creative, Translation Vendors

## Purpose

Macys serves customers across the continental US, Puerto Rico, and a small but growing Canadian footprint. Campaign copy must adapt to regional language, holidays, and pricing without losing brand voice. This guide defines the expectations.

## Spanish Localization (Texas, Florida, Puerto Rico)

All Texas, Florida, and Puerto Rico campaigns must ship a Spanish version alongside English. The Spanish version is not a literal translation. It is a transcreation that preserves brand voice, warmth, and intent.

Key conventions:

* Use neutral Latin American Spanish, not Castilian
* Star Rewards is translated as "Premios Star" (a fixed translation, do not vary)
* "Up to 40 percent off" translates to "Hasta 40 por ciento de descuento"
* "Starting at 10 percent off" translates to "Desde 10 por ciento de descuento"
* Avoid English borrowings where a clean Spanish equivalent exists (use "oferta" rather than "deal")
* Puerto Rico copy should reference local holidays where the calendar overlaps (see Regional Holidays below)

Translations are reviewed by the in market Macys Spanish editor, not by an outside vendor alone, before campaign launch. Following the Q4 2025 Holiday typo incident in Puerto Rico signage, Spanish creative is now reviewed twice, once at copy lock and once at final asset before any print or production run.

## French Canadian

Any creative running in Quebec or in cross border campaigns reaching Canadian customers requires a French Canadian version. Use Quebec French, not France French. Key conventions:

* Star Rewards translates as "Recompenses Star"
* Pricing follows Canadian dollar with currency code (CAD)
* Quebec consumer protection rules require both languages on any paid out of home placement

## Regional Pricing

Pricing in Puerto Rico is set in US dollars but may differ from continental US pricing on certain categories (notably Beauty and Fragrance) due to shipping and tariff costs. Always pull the regional price from the Pricing Strategy regional table, never assume the continental price applies.

Canadian pricing is in CAD and follows the Canada Pricing table maintained by Pricing Strategy.

## Star Rewards Tier Names by Region

* Continental US: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum
* Puerto Rico: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platino
* Canada (English): Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum
* Canada (French): Bronze, Argent, Or, Platine

The Platino spelling for Puerto Rico is intentional and customer tested. Do not "correct" it back to Platinum.

## Regional Holidays

The following holidays shift creative calendar planning by region. Confirm with Regional Marketing before scheduling campaigns near these dates.

* Three Kings Day (January 6) in Puerto Rico, treated as a primary gifting holiday equal to or larger than Christmas Eve. Creative should include Three Kings imagery and a gifting angle.
* Lunar New Year (date varies, late January or February) on the West Coast, particularly the San Francisco Bay Area and Greater Los Angeles. Beauty and Home see the largest lift.
* Dia de los Muertos (November 1 and 2) in Texas, Arizona, New Mexico, and Southern California, primarily a Home and Beauty moment.
* Quebec National Holiday (June 24) for any Canadian creative running through late June.
