# Campaign Approval Policy

**Document ID:** APPRV-POL-2025-003
**Owner:** Marketing Operations
**Last updated:** November 8, 2025
**Audience:** All Marketing, Brand, Legal, Merchandising

## Purpose

This policy defines who must review and approve a campaign before it goes live. The intent is to keep approval predictable, reduce last minute escalations, and make sure the right experts see the right work at the right time.

## Standard Approval Tiers

Every campaign moves through the following baseline reviewers in order:

1. Marketing Manager (the campaign owner submits)
2. Brand Review (voice, visual, tagline compliance)
3. Channel Lead (email, paid social, site, print as applicable)
4. Marketing Director sign off

A campaign cannot move to production until all four baseline tiers have signed off.

## Special Case Reviewers

The following triggers add reviewers on top of the baseline tiers.

### Spend Threshold

Campaigns with total media spend over $500,000 require VP of Marketing plus Legal review. The VP review focuses on portfolio fit and budget allocation. Legal focuses on disclosure and pricing claims.

### New SKUs

Any campaign featuring a SKU that has not previously appeared in Macys marketing creative within the previous 24 months requires Merchandising sign off. Merchandising confirms inventory depth, pricing alignment, and vendor MAP compliance.

### Regulated Regions

Campaigns running in New York or California require Legal review regardless of spend. Both states have heightened consumer protection statutes (the New York Attorney General settlement on "compare at" pricing being the most cited reference) and Legal must clear all pricing language before launch.

### Sweepstakes or Contest

Any campaign that includes a prize, drawing, or contest mechanic requires Legal plus Compliance review. Bonded states (FL, NY, RI) require additional filings handled by Legal.

## Service Level Agreements

* Standard approval: 3 business days from submission to final sign off
* High spend approval (over $500K): 5 business days
* Sweepstakes approval: 7 business days (allow time for bond filings)
* Regulated region approval: 5 business days

If a reviewer does not respond within the window, the campaign owner should escalate to the reviewer's manager, then to Marketing Operations.

## Escalation Path

Stuck approvals escalate in this order:

1. Reviewer's direct manager
2. Marketing Operations approval queue lead
3. VP of Marketing
4. CMO (only for issues stalled more than 10 business days)

## Out of Hours

Reviewers are not expected to approve campaigns outside business hours. Campaigns submitted Friday after 3 PM ET are considered received the following Monday.

## Pre Clearance

The single biggest predictor of a clean approval cycle is pre clearing contentious items with Brand and Legal at campaign kickoff, before the first creative draft is built. Pre clearance is not a substitute for formal review, but it reduces rework dramatically and is strongly recommended for any campaign with a multiplier offer, comparative claim, financing offer, or new tagline.
