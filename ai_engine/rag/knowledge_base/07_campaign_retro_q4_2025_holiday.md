# Q4 2025 Holiday Campaign Retrospective

**Document ID:** RETRO-Q4-2025
**Owner:** Holiday Campaign Lead, Marketing Operations
**Last updated:** January 15, 2026
**Audience:** Marketing, Brand, Creative, Operations

## Summary

The Q4 2025 Holiday campaign closed with mixed results. Revenue against plan came in at 98 percent, a small miss driven by softer than expected Home performance in the final two weeks. The wins, misses, and action items below come from the retro session held January 12, 2026, with representatives from Marketing, Brand, Creative, Legal, and Merchandising.

## Wins

### Beauty Loyalists Segment Outperformed

The Beauty Loyalists segment (customers who purchased prestige Beauty in the prior 12 months and held an active Star Rewards account) outperformed plan by 23 percent. Email open rate hit 41 percent (versus a 28 percent target) and conversion ran 1.6x the segment average. The team attributes this to the early November teaser series, which front loaded gift sets before the broader Holiday push and avoided the late November inbox crush.

### Cross Channel Consistency

Holiday creative held a consistent look across email, paid social, site, and out of home. The Brand audit scored the campaign at 4.6 out of 5, the highest score for any Holiday in three years.

### Star Rewards Multiplier Drove Repeat

The 5x Star Rewards weekend (December 6 through 8) drove a 34 percent lift in repeat purchase rate among existing members. Legal pre cleared the disclaimer two weeks ahead, which avoided the late approval risk seen in prior years.

## Misses

### DAM Degradation Delayed Production

Four key Beauty assets uploaded in late October were flagged as low resolution mid November. Creative had to rebuild them, which pushed the second wave of email creative four days late and forced a rush approval cycle. Root cause was a DAM ingest issue where source files were down sampled on upload. Creative Operations confirmed the underlying ingest bug is patched but the team lost four production days.

### Two Disclaimers Flagged Late

Legal flagged two late stage disclaimers in the gift card creative (insufficient Truth in Lending disclosure on the deferred financing offer) on December 1. The fix required pulling the creative for 18 hours and reissuing. Postmortem traced this to a copy review that bypassed Legal during a holiday week stand in.

### Spanish Localization Typos

The Puerto Rico Spanish creative shipped with two typos in the headline (a missing accent on "ofertas" and an incorrect verb conjugation in the call to action). The market editor caught both, but the assets had already gone to print on a small run of in store signage, which had to be reprinted at a cost of approximately $12,000.

## Action Items

1. DAM ingest validation. Creative Operations will require a resolution check at upload and reject assets below the production threshold. Owner: Creative Operations. Due: March 31, 2026.

2. Standing Legal review during holiday windows. Legal commits to a named primary reviewer plus a named backup for every holiday week, with handoff documented in writing. Owner: Marketing Counsel. Due: October 1, 2026 (ahead of Q4 2026).

3. Two stage Spanish review. All Spanish creative must be reviewed by the in market Macys Spanish editor twice, once at copy lock and once at final asset before any print or production run. Owner: Localization Council. Due: immediate, starting February 2026 campaigns.

4. Comparative claim methodology library. Build a reusable methodology library for year over year claims so future campaigns are not blocked by Legal on the basis of missing comparison data. Owner: Marketing Counsel plus Marketing Operations. Due: May 1, 2026.
