# Legal Disclaimer Requirements for Campaign Creative

**Document ID:** LEGAL-DIS-2026-002
**Owner:** Legal, Marketing Counsel
**Last updated:** February 14, 2026
**Audience:** Marketing, Creative, Copywriting

## Purpose

This document lists the legal disclaimers that must appear in Macys campaign creative. It is updated as regulations change. When in doubt, route the campaign to Legal review before publishing.

## Star Rewards

Any creative that mentions Star Rewards points, tier benefits, or member exclusive offers must include the following at minimum:

"Star Rewards program subject to terms. See macys.com/starrewards for details."

If the campaign mentions a specific points multiplier (for example, "earn 5x points"), the qualifying categories and time window must appear in the same disclaimer block. Example:

"Earn 5x Star Rewards points on Beauty purchases. Valid March 1 through March 31, 2026. See terms at macys.com/starrewards."

## Macys Credit Card and Financing

Any mention of the Macys Credit Card, deferred interest, or zero percent financing requires the following disclaimer in the same creative unit:

"Subject to credit approval. See application for important details on rates, fees, terms, and conditions. Credit issued by Citibank, N.A."

For deferred interest offers, the additional Truth in Lending Act language must appear: "Interest will be charged to your account from the purchase date if the promotional balance is not paid in full within the promotional period."

## Pricing Claims

### Up To Claims

Any "up to X percent off" claim must include the minimum discount available in the offer. Example: "Up to 40 percent off, starting at 10 percent off."

This is required by Federal Trade Commission guidance on representative pricing claims.

### Compare At Pricing

Following the 2024 New York Attorney General settlement, any "compare at" or "original price" claim must reflect a price at which the item was offered for a meaningful period within the prior 90 days. If the comparison price was a manufacturer suggested retail price rather than a Macys offered price, the disclaimer must read:

"Compare at price reflects the manufacturer suggested retail price. Item may not have been sold at this price."

## Comparative Claims

Claims that compare Macys pricing to competitors (for example, "save more than at competitor X") are not permitted without prior Legal review and documentation of the comparison methodology. Legal must approve the source data and the time window for the comparison.

## Endorsement Disclosures

Any creative featuring a celebrity, influencer, or partner endorsement must include the connection disclosure:

"Paid partnership" or "Macys partner" tag on social posts and a visible disclosure on landing pages.

If the endorser received free product, the disclosure must read "Gifted by Macys" instead of "Paid partnership."

## Sweepstakes

Sweepstakes creative must include the following:

* No purchase necessary
* Void where prohibited
* Odds depend on number of entries
* End date and time including time zone
* Full rules link

## Sizing of Disclaimers

Disclaimers must be at least 8 point on print, 10 point on digital, and clearly legible against background. Disclaimers may not be hidden, faded, or placed off canvas. On video, disclaimers must remain on screen for at least 4 seconds and be paired with a stable background color where possible.

## Escalation

When Legal cannot resolve a disclaimer question within the SLA, escalate to the Marketing Counsel lead. For matters touching active settlements (the New York compare at settlement, the California financing review), escalate to Marketing Counsel plus the Compliance Officer.
